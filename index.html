Information in the app

Car Loop, Cube Hit, and Color Flow do not require an account and do not collect, upload, sell, or share personal information or gameplay data from the apps. Their current releases do not integrate advertising, analytics, attribution, remote crash-reporting, or in-app purchase services, and do not access an advertising identifier.

Game progress, level state, coins, lives, tool quantities, and sound or vibration preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not receive a server copy of your game save.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app storage, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove that data. Device or operating-system backup settings may affect whether a backup copy remains. Your platform provider controls those backups. We cannot recover a save that has been deleted locally.

If you contact support, we retain your correspondence only as needed to handle your request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of that correspondence.

Website visits and external links

Opening a policy or support page uses your external browser. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, the hosting and delivery services receive normal connection information, such as your IP address, requested page, browser information, and security logs. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The domain uses Alibaba Cloud DNS. This website does not include advertising or audience-analytics scripts. Hosting and infrastructure information may be processed in the countries where the providers operate. External sites and platform services have their own privacy policies.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we and our email provider process your email address, message, and any attachments to respond to your request. You choose what to send. We do not sell support correspondence.

Children

Car Loop, Cube Hit, and Color Flow are intended for people aged 13 and older and are not directed to children under 13. The current apps do not collect personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to support, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

Security

Your device and operating system protect your local save. We use HTTPS for this website and limit our use of support information to handling requests and meeting legal obligations. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and its effective date if covered apps or their data practices change. You can reopen the policy from the app settings.
